We would like to inform you of an important update regarding your order of seed bombs.

In order to significantly improve germination rates and overall success, we have recently developed and adopted pre-stratified milkweed seeds. This process mimics natural germination conditions and allows the seeds to sprout more easily at the right time.

For this reason, milkweed seed bombs will now be replaced with pre-stratified milkweed seeds, which we consider a more effective and better-suited option for supporting the growth of this essential plant for pollinators.

Additionally, we are currently facing certain challenges related to shipping seed bombs to the United States, due to stricter regulations regarding the import of soil and soil-based products. This change also allows us to ensure more reliable and compliant delivery.

If you would prefer not to receive pre-stratified seeds, you may cancel your order before it is shipped. Please feel free to contact us promptly if this is the case.

We sincerely thank you for your understanding and for your commitment to protecting pollinators.
