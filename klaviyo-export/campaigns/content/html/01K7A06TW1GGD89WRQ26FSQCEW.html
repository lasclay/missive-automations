This plant, also known as North American silk, grows wild across our continent and—almost poetically—opens its seed pods to release its silky fibers right when the weather turns cold, right when we need that extra warmth. Mother Nature really does think of everything!
Our beanies, neck warmers, headbands and scarves make full use of the insulating power of milkweed silk to withstand the cool autumn weather. They retain body heat, repel the chilly winds and retain no condensation, thanks to milkweed's hydrophobic properties.
