- **Original**

- **Réponse rédigée**
  > Honestly, we have never seen a study that follows that particular swap, so we are not going to invent numbers for you 🌿 What we can say is that the two plants get confused far more often than they should. Japanese knotweed is a foreign wrecker; common milkweed is native, vigorous and simply refuses to stay in its lane. On a cleared, sunny patch, milkweed does best going in as a clump rather than one lonely stem. If you try it, come tell us how it turns out 🦋
