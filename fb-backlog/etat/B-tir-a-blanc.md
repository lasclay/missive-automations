## 1. Semis tardif en Géorgie

- **id** `122160870794483418_1904141433675508` · 2025-06-27
- **Original**
  > I'm in the Atlanta GA area. Can I still plant milkweed in late June / early July?
- **Réponse rédigée**
  > Late June is a difficult moment to start milkweed from seed. Ours is not stratified, and it needs roughly 30 days of cold before it germinates reliably, so a summer sowing usually disappoints. Two ways around it: give the seed a month in the fridge and start it in pots, or wait and sow directly in the fall, and let winter do the cold for you. Either way, expect foliage the first year and flowers the second.
- **Thème** 5, « Quand et comment semer / stratification » (graines non stratifiées, ~30 jours de froid, semis d'automne, floraison à la 2e année).

## 2. Bombes semencières

- **id** `122142476066483418_610922611815565` · 2025-03-12
- **Original**
  > Lasclay: The Milkweed Company how do the seed bombs work?
- **Réponse rédigée**
  > Plant them as soon as they arrive. Each one already carries its own soil mix, seed and moisture, so there is nothing to prepare: press them into the ground in a sunny spot and leave them alone. If yours have already sprouted inside the package, start them in small pots under clear plastic instead, so they keep their humidity while they settle in.

## 3. Quelle espèce pour le Missouri

- **id** `122191968992483418_2186079912139304` · 2026-05-05
- **Original**
  > You do not specify which variety is native. I live in Missouri. What do you suggest?
- **Réponse rédigée**
  > All eleven species we carry are North American natives, and none of them is tropical milkweed. For Missouri, the usual starting points are common milkweed (syriaca), butterfly weed (tuberosa) and swamp milkweed (incarnata): the first spreads by rhizome and suits an open or naturalized spot, the other two stay where you put them and behave in a garden bed. Worth checking a Missouri native plant list before ordering, since a local range map is more precise than anything we can tell you from here.
- **Thème** 2, « Quelle espèce pour ma région » (11 espèces indigènes, aucune tropicale, renvoi vers une source locale).

## 4. Lavage d'une tuque et de mitaines

- **Original**

  > I presume it washes well?
- **Réponse rédigée**
  > It does. Cold water, delicate cycle, mild soap, then dry flat in open air. The one thing to avoid is fabric softener, which coats the fibre and costs it part of its insulating power. Toques and mittens both exist already on lasclay.com, and the product-by-product care guides are at lasclay.com/pages/guides-dentretien.
