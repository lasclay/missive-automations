## À LIRE AVANT : ce document n'est pas un dossier de presse

Tu m'as demandé de le joindre aux 21 courriels français. Il est déjà parti dans
les brouillons. Avant d'appuyer sur envoyer, sache ce qu'un journaliste y lit :

**Planche 7** : le graphique des ventes mensuelles 2020-2025, avec les pointes de
novembre et décembre 2025 autour de 200 000 $ et 270 000 $.

**Planche 10** : « nous nous sommes financés à bout de bras, à même notre fonds de
roulement, nous causant de fréquents enjeux de liquidités ».

**Planche 11** : le projet de déplacer la fabrication des accessoires les plus
populaires en Tunisie, et le besoin d'augmenter le fonds de roulement.

**Planche 13** : le chiffre d'affaires de Sounds Good, celui de Lasclay passé de
250 000 $ à 1 M$, et ton lymphome.

C'est un document d'investisseurs. Tout y est vrai et rien n'est honteux, mais
« une PME québécoise en manque de liquidités déménage sa production en Tunisie »
est un titre que ces planches écrivent toutes seules, deux jours avant ta
diffusion. Le garde-fou de la marque est explicite là-dessus : on ne publie ni
états financiers, ni marges, ni projections.

**Trois options.** Envoyer tel quel et assumer. Retirer les planches 7, 10, 11 et
le dernier paragraphe de la 13, ce qui donne un vrai dossier de presse en neuf
planches. Ou ne rien joindre du tout et laisser le média kit faire la job.

Je peux produire la version courte en une commande, dis-moi.

## La traduction, planche par planche

### 1. Couverture
**PRÉSENTATION D'ENTREPRISE** → **COMPANY PRESENTATION**

### 2. ASCLÉPIADE : UN TRÉSOR OUBLIÉ DE NOTRE TERROIR
**MILKWEED: A FORGOTTEN TREASURE OF OUR OWN SOIL**

- We are a young, vertically integrated company built around milkweed, a native plant with
  broad industrial potential and real environmental benefits.
- We develop technologies and products that put its singular fibre to work.
- We are devoted to this plant, and we want to make it a Quebec signature recognized worldwide.
- In doing so, we help sustain its cultivation, which creates habitat for the threatened
  monarch butterfly.

### 3. UNIQUE, INÉDIT, PROMETTEUR
**UNPRECEDENTED, AND FULL OF PROMISE**

- Long treated as a weed to be wiped out, milkweed is now farmed at scale by Quebec growers:
  a project without equivalent anywhere in the world.
- Since 2020 we have brought more than 50 products to market from the two most promising parts
  of the plant, its seeds and its floss.
- Sought after by home gardeners and also containing a moisturizing, antioxidant oil, seeds
  account for roughly 25% of our revenue.
- Highly insulating, light, water-repellent and antibacterial, milkweed floss is our flagship
  raw material: it goes into garments and accessories that account for roughly 70% of our revenue.

### 4. UN MÉCANISME DE CONSERVATION ÉPROUVÉ
**A CONSERVATION MECHANISM THAT HAS ALREADY WORKED**

Graphique : *Évolution de la taille des colonies de monarques au Mexique (en hectares)* →
**Size of monarch colonies in Mexico (in hectares)**. Repère : *Démarrage de l'industrie de
l'asclépiade au Québec* → **Quebec milkweed industry begins**. Source: World Wildlife
Fund-México, Radio-Canada.

- After coming close to extinction in 2014, eastern monarch populations reached their highest
  level in 20 years following the planting of more than 1,000 hectares of milkweed in Quebec,
  Ontario and the United States.
- This emblematic, threatened species migrates from the Oyamel fir forests of Mexico up to
  Canada, following the milkweed as it emerges. It then breeds all summer across five
  generations before heading back to Mexico in the fall, ahead of the milkweed harvest.
- Large milkweed plantations are an unexpected new habitat for them, and a rare win-win use of
  a natural resource for both nature and people.

### 5. UNE PLANTE ANCIENNE, DES ENJEUX MODERNES
**AN OLD PLANT, A MODERN PROBLEM**

Légende de l'image : *Soies d'asclépiade au microscope: 80% vides, les fibres emmagasinent l'air
et crée une puissante barrière thermique, acoustique et imperméable, même avec une mince couche*
→ **Milkweed floss under the microscope: 80% hollow, the fibres trap air and create a powerful
thermal, acoustic and water-resistant barrier, even in a thin layer.**

- Everyone wants products that are local, responsible, ethical and effective, but few want to
  pay much more to get them.
- Lasclay sits at the rare intersection of those constraints: affordability, environmental
  responsibility, ethics, local origin and technical performance.
- Where many have failed to commercialize milkweed, we hold our ground through a vertically
  integrated model that keeps sourcing, design, research, manufacturing, marketing and
  distribution working together.

### 6. UNE PASSION AUTHENTIQUE QUI SÉDUIT DES MILLIERS D'ADEPTES
**A GENUINE OBSESSION THAT HAS WON OVER THOUSANDS**

- From the start, our devotion to milkweed, our way of talking about it and our product range
  have won over tens of thousands of followers.
- Our community of more than 60,000 subscribers, 36,000 of them customers, together with our
  R&D work, has produced steady growth that has accelerated sharply over the past year.

### 7. UN ENGOUEMENT INTENSE QUI S'ACCENTUE
**DEMAND IS BUILDING, AND IT IS SPEEDING UP**

Titre du graphique : *VENTES MENSUELLES LASCLAY 2020-2025* → **LASCLAY MONTHLY SALES 2020-2025**.
Repères : **NOV 2025**, **DEC 2025**.

### 8. DES PRODUITS DIVERSIFIÉS, PERFORMANTS ET POPULAIRES
**A RANGE THAT PERFORMS, AND THAT SELLS**

- Our range falls into three main categories: winter clothing, insulated summer and all-season
  products such as coolers, and regenerative horticulture.
- Since 2020 we have sold more than 10,000 pairs of mitts and 5,000 neck warmers, our two
  best-selling textile products.
- We have also distributed more than 8 million milkweed seeds across North America, 30 times
  more than the David Suzuki Foundation, the organization most involved in monarch protection.

### 9. UNE DÉBROUILLARDISE À L'IMAGE DE L'ASCLÉPIADE
**AS RESOURCEFUL AS THE PLANT ITSELF**

**Natural inspiration.** Milkweed grows anywhere, even in sidewalk cracks. It adapts to what
surrounds it and performs on very little.

**In-house innovation.** Inspired by that resilience, we developed a whole set of unheard-of
industrial processes in-house and on a shoestring, which let us make milkweed products faster
than anyone.

**Advanced manufacturing.** CNC cutting, moulding, thermal lamination, robotic sewing, 3D
printing, laser cutting. We invented and automated several textile manufacturing processes that
many people thought impossible.

### 10. DE GRANDS DÉFIS POUR DE GRANDS RÊVES
**BIG DREAMS COME WITH BIG PROBLEMS**

- Supply is immature, precarious and limited: the milkweed harvest is hard to mechanize and
  highly perishable. We have found inventive, dependable growers who give us quality, but they
  are far from devoting themselves to it full time.
- We have been able to grow by adapting our model to squeeze the most value out of small volumes
  of milkweed, through small accessories, seed products and the like.
- But to build our production infrastructure, develop a wide range and get out of seasonality,
  we financed ourselves the hard way, out of working capital, which brings on frequent liquidity
  problems.

### 11. PROCHAINES ÉTAPES & CONDITIONS POUR ACCÉLÉRER
**WHAT COMES NEXT, AND WHAT IT WILL TAKE**

- In 2025 we launched our first milkweed coats. They generated more than $50,000 in sales in a
  single day, confirming that the enthusiasm reaches beyond small accessories and can carry more
  expensive products, which opens a great deal up for us.
- The coats were our first product made partly abroad, in Tunisia, and their success has us
  considering moving production of our most popular accessories there as well, to drive both
  local and international growth.
- Sleeping bags, bedding, parkas, gloves. We can also consider commercializing every large or
  more complex product that is in high demand and that we could not make in Quebec for technical
  or financial reasons.
- International manufacturing brings its own difficulties, liquidity above all, since deposits
  come months in advance. Beyond increasing our working capital for that, we will also need to
  improve our production management, most likely through a solid ERP.
- Growing milkweed ourselves is also on the table, to secure the supply of our flagship material,
  cut costs and open new revenue streams, for instance field visits with a shop, or ending our
  purchases of other species' seeds in the United States.

### 12. NOTRE VISION POUR L'ASCLÉPIADE
**OUR VISION FOR MILKWEED**

- We want to make milkweed a Quebec signature recognized worldwide, much as Icebreaker did for
  merino wool in New Zealand.
- But we want to go further: turn the milkweed industry into a powerful restoration lever for
  threatened monarchs, and into a way to make marginal North American farmland worth something.
- Milkweed is not a luxury material. It has the potential to be the most affordable and durable
  insulating material in the world. We can democratize it by maximizing its use in affordable
  products while developing new high-value outlets.
- Lasclay works directly at the junctions of the value chain, to clear the blockages that choke
  the industry. By reconnecting those links, it secures supply, makes large-scale restoration
  possible, and along the way develops singular technologies that can be sold across the textile
  industry.

### 13. À PROPOS DU FONDATEUR GABRIEL GOUVEIA
**ABOUT FOUNDER GABRIEL GOUVEIA**

A devoted forest forager, I discovered milkweed in 2016 while prototyping compostable food
containers from milkweed fibre and Japanese knotweed pulled from eradication campaigns against
that invasive species. The project took second place worldwide at UQAM's international PackPlay2
competition and was shown at the 2017 World Design Summit in Montreal.

After my bachelor's in industrial design at Université Laval, I turned to entrepreneurship in
2019, during treatment for Hodgkin lymphoma. That ordeal convinced me not to put my plans off,
and I co-founded Sounds Good, an audio electronics brand I helped grow from $5,000 to $1.2M in
annual revenue in two years.

After selling my shares and buying out Lasclay in 2022, I devoted myself fully to giving milkweed
its moment: scaling and partly automating our manufacturing technologies, taking the catalogue
from 2 to more than 50 products, and raising annual direct sales from $250,000 to $1M in three
years.

## Le prompt, si tu passes par un outil qui voit les images

À coller tel quel dans ChatGPT ou Gemini, avec le PDF et le texte ci-dessus.

```
Voici une présentation d'entreprise de 13 planches, en français, exportée
d'InDesign. Je te fournis la traduction anglaise validée de chaque planche,
ci-dessous.

Ta tâche : reproduire chaque planche à l'identique, en remplaçant uniquement le
texte français par la traduction anglaise fournie. Ne traduis rien toi-même,
utilise exactement les chaînes que je te donne.

Contraintes fermes :
- Ne change aucune illustration, photo, icône, couleur, ni la position des blocs.
- Garde la police, la graisse et la casse d'origine. Les titres sont en
  majuscules, condensés.
- Le logo Lasclay et le nom de la marque restent identiques, jamais traduits.
- « Asclépiade » devient « milkweed », jamais « asclepias » ni « silkweed ».
- Les montants restent en dollars canadiens avec le même formatage.
- Si un bloc anglais est plus long que le français, réduis le corps du texte
  plutôt que de déborder ou de recadrer l'image.
- La planche 7 et la planche 4 contiennent des graphiques : ne traduis que le
  titre, les étiquettes d'axe et les annotations, jamais les données.

Rends-moi un fichier par planche, en 3440 x 1920 px, puis un PDF assemblé.
```

**Ce qui marchera mal, et pourquoi.** Un modèle d'image regénère l'illustration au
lieu de la copier : les aquarelles, le monarque, les gousses vont changer. Pour un
rendu fidèle, le seul chemin propre est de rouvrir l'InDesign et de remplacer les
blocs de texte par les chaînes ci-dessus. C'est une heure de travail pour
quelqu'un qui a le fichier source, et le résultat est exact.
