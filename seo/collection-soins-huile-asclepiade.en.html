More about milkweed seed oil and this collection

Why milkweed seed oil for skin and hair?

Pressing milkweed seeds for their oil puts to use what is left once the floss, our insulation, has been harvested. As little as possible goes to waste, and the same plant feeds the monarch caterpillar: every field grown for the fibre is one more habitat. That is the logic behind the whole collection, and the reason we ended up making soap as well as mittens.

An oil that is rare in the plant world

Milkweed seed oil is rich in omega-6 and omega-9 fatty acids, and in omega-7, uncommon among plants. It also contains 5 of the 8 forms of vitamin E, including tocotrienols, known for their antioxidant and moisturizing properties. Its colour is golden and its fresh, green scent recalls camelina oil. To understand where the plant comes from, read our page on milkweed.
