Free exchanges in Canada

Wrong size, wrong colour? We exchange it for free, anywhere in Canada: prepaid return label, no handling fee. You have 15 days after delivery to write to us, and the item must be in new condition, unworn, unwashed, with no odour or marks.

  1. Write to hey@lasclay.com or on Messenger with your order number and the item you would like in exchange.
  2. You receive a prepaid return label within 1 to 2 business days. You then have 10 days to mail the parcel.
  3. Drop it in a mailbox or at a Canada Post counter. The replacement ships as soon as we receive and inspect yours.

Not sure about your size before ordering? Check the size guide, or send us a photo of your hand next to a ruler and we will confirm the size.

Returns and refunds

Would you rather have a refund? Same 15-day window, same new condition, same prepaid label. A $9.99 handling fee is deducted from the refund; it covers part of the postage. The refund goes back to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days after inspection.

What is not eligible

  • Final sale items and items from the Imperfects section: no returns or exchanges.
  • Purchases made anywhere other than lasclay.com: please contact the retailer.
  • Outside Canada: write to us and we will find a solution, but return shipping costs are not covered.

A defect?

A seam coming apart, a missing part, the wrong item received: write to us with one or two photos and your order number, even after the return window. In most cases we sort it out without asking you to send the product back.

Shipping

Canada. Free Xpresspost shipping on orders of $99 and up. Under that amount, $6.99 for standard shipping or $9.99 for Xpresspost. Very light items, such as seeds, ship by stamp for $2.99, without tracking. Delivery times after shipping: 2 to 5 business days in Québec, 4 to 8 in Ontario and the Maritimes, 6 to 8 in Manitoba, 7 to 10 in Saskatchewan, Alberta and British Columbia.

United States. $6.99 USD on orders under $59.99 USD, free on orders of $59.99 USD and up.

Elsewhere. United Kingdom, France, Belgium, Switzerland, Australia, New Zealand and Mexico: rates are shown in the cart based on the destination.

Pre-order products ship after the date shown on their product page. You receive an email with the tracking number when your order ships.
