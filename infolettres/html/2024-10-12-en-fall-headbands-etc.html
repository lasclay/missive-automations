We hope this letter finds you well, as Fall is bringing back its flamboyant colors, crisp air and indescribable fragrance.

It seems that little pleasures multiply and become more noticeable during this wonderful season... The smell of fallen leaves, the invigorating freshness, the effervescence back-to-school/back-to-work, steaming soups that warm the soul, shorter days that make us a little nostalgic, getting cosy inside on a rainy weekend, the last outdoor campfires, hot drinks after a long walk, the slow cooker making a comeback...
It's all these little universal scenes, deeply rooted in life on our land, that we have in mind when we design our milkweed-insulated accessories. This plant, also known as American silk, grows abundantly on our territory and, in a quasi-poetic manner, opens its fruits and releases its silks just when it starts to get chilly, just when the need to keep warm increases. It seems Mother Nature thinks things through!
 
Our beanies, neck warmers, headbands and scarves make full use of the power of milkweed silk to withstand the cool autumn temperatures. They retain body heat, repel the chilly winds and retain no condensation, thanks to milkweed's hydrophobic properties. 

Everything is made right here in Canada, from harvest to your hands.
