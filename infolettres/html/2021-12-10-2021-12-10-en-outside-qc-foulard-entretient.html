Here are some tips and tricks to help you use the scarf properly and maximize its longevity:

  • We recommend that you wear the scarf flat, avoid twisting it and making knots in it. The milkweed inside has a fairly good hold, but like any quilted item, it should be treated with delicacy (see photos below for reference).
  • Cleaning: we recommend hand washing in cold water with a mild soap. Do not wring or rub excessively. To wring out, it is possible to place the scarf flat between a towel and squeeze gently. It should not be put in the washing machine, as the centrifugal force deteriorates the milkweed's hold in the scarf.
  • Drying: Lay flat to dry, or on a hanger. Do not put it in the dryer.
  • Washing frequency: If steps 2 and 3 seem tedious, good news! Both milkweed and bamboo viscose are naturally antibacterial and/or hypoallergenic (milkweed) fibers. It is therefore not necessary to wash the scarf very often. 1 time per season would be enough!
  • It is possible that your scarf still has a certain odor coming from the fact that we have just finished crafting them in our workshop in order to send them in time for the holidays. We wanted to reassure you, however, that the smell is not permanent and will fade after a few days.
  • It is possible that your scarf feels a bit stiff at first. This is because of the way we hold the milkweed in place. By wearing the scarf a few times, it will soften naturally.
