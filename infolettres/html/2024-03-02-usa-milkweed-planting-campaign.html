For Lasclay, in the last four years, the month of March marks the start of our National Milkweed Planting Campaign across Canada and the USA.
 
This magnificent plant, grown commercially for its insulating silk, is also an environmental cornerstone that has been bringing serious benefits to our ecosystems for millions of years:
  • Milkweed is the exclusive host plant of the iconic monarch butterfly, which is threatened with extinction precisely because of the scarcity of milkweed in North America.
  • It is an important food source for several specialized insects and native pollinators.
  • Milkweed farming is an effective and sustainable way to sequester large amounts of carbon dioxide from the atmosphere through photosynthesis, to fight climate change.
The start of large-scale milkweed farming in 2013 has compounded these environmental benefits, but it’s still far from sufficient to ensure the survival of the monarchs, which our company has made its main mission.
 

 

Monarchs need more milkweed, and they need it everywhere. In the countryside, in the suburbs, in the cities.

The idea of a nationwide planting campaign was born when we saw the huge amounts of seeds from the milkweed silk extraction process, which until then had been unused.

By redistributing them across the country, thousands of milkweed gardens have been emerging from the ground since 2021: a good start for monarchs, for whom Canada is the final migratory destination each spring after flying across the United States from Mexico.

And this year, we have great news.

Knowing that there were other, smaller, and less hardy native species of milkweed than the Common variety we grow for silk, we worked hard to produce and bag seeds of butterfly, swamp and showy milkweed (tuberosa, incarnata & speciosa), which are beautiful and just as beloved by monarchs.

We are very proud to launch our 2024 National Milkweed Planting Campaign by introducing these three new varieties in seed packet format!

The only thing monarchs love more than a milkweed garden is a garden with several species of milkweed and native flowers.
