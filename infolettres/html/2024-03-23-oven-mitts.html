In our last communications, we hinted at a new kitchen-related mystery product to be launched soon. We're very proud and pleased to announce not one, but three new milkweed-insulated products:

  • Oven mitts, offering unrivalled protection against heat, while protecting the environment and biodiversity.

  • A small pair of isothermal potholders, to place your small dishes on the counter or table without damaging them, and can also be used as potholders to take food out of the oven without burning your hands.

  • A large trivet, perfect for placing your best concoctions in the center of the table, without damaging it.
Some may wonder why Lasclay has suddenly branched out into kitchen products, after having become known mainly for our outdoor accessories. The reason is quite simple. I was inspired by two things: firstly, my family history, and secondly, my deep and unshakeable conviction that milkweed has much more to offer than insulating outdoor accessories.

If you have a few minutes on this beautiful Saturday, let me tell you the story of my grandfather Eduardo, an immigrant who came to Canada from the Portuguese archipelago of the Azores in the 1950s. He was an exceptional baker. Rejected on his first attempt at immigration because his job was considered undesirable in Canada, he then rolled his hands in pebbles, bricks and dirt for several weeks. On his second attempt, he simply showed his flayed hands and claimed he was a bricklayer, and he passed!

We could say it was white lie, because his receiving country would have missed something... and I'm not talking about Lasclay, who obviously wouldn't exist, but about Eduardo's extraordinary gifts. Quickly hired in a bakery and pastry shop on his arrival, he quickly impressed his bosses. It was even said that he knew if a loaf of bread would rise just by touching it. A workaholic, he operated the stoves day and night,  at work and at home, to feed hungry mouths. 
My grand-father Eduardo, working the ovens.

My grandfather passed away a long time ago, but he left my mother Maria Eduarda his talent for baking marvels. From a very early age, I would accompany him to bake Massa Sovada, a delicious, brioche-like, sweet, lemon-scented Portuguese bread. The Massa Sovada was an epic, day-long family adventure in which my 3 brothers and I accompanied our mother. It had to be kneaded in a huge bowl, and at 5 feet tall, despite all her enthusiasm, she ended up lacking the arm length and strength to finish the kneading!

Stories like these demonstrate the rich depth, sometimes ordinary, sometimes extraordinary, that lies in the daily routine of family meals and cooking, with all the rituals they entail.

We see something similar with milkweed. It's commonplace, ordinary and taken for granted, but it reveals its extraordinary beauty to anyone who takes the trouble to look closely. It's not a luxury item. It's a plant with a thousand virtues, which deserves to be better known and democratized. At Lasclay, we've made this our mission.

Democratizing milkweed starts by making it part of our daily rituals, part of our lives, close to us every day.

These are the inspirations that have led us to launch milkweed kitchen products, which unlike our outdoor accessories, can be used all year round, in the heart of our homes, in our daily lives, in what we hold most sacred: making great food with our loved ones.

We hope you'll be as inspired by this idea as we are, and that you'll now entrust milkweed with the task of protecting you from the heat of your next freshly baked apple pie.

We've only produced a hundred units of each product. Hurry, they'll go like hotcakes (or Portuguese sweet bread)!
