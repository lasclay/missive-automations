We've been looking forward to writing back to our community, following a third season that was just as intense and rewarding as the previous ones. During this time, we welcomed our first 10 employees (yes, 10!), pushed our manufacturing innovations into uncharted territory, and yes, had to deal with some major production delays, something that hadn't happened to us since our first season in 2020.

The main reasons for these delays:

  • A very high demand for our milkweed products that we are struggling to meet, despite all of our recent innovation and hirings.

  • The split between the original founders of Lasclay in the Summer of 2022, a situation we chose to keep private until now. Now alone at the head of Lasclay, Gabriel had to move the factory and start over in September. By the time everything was ready to start production, it was already November 8. In just one month and a half (to December 20), Lasclay surpassed last year’s production totals, manufactured from August 2021 to February 2022. A feat we are very proud of under the circumstances, but one we will obviously avoid in the future!

Now that the dust has settled, we can start looking ahead again, as time is running out for the monarch butterflies. In addition to being officially declared an endangered species in July 2022, a recent World Wildlife Fund census measured a further 22% decrease in their population from last year. Our mission is the same: to bring back monarch populations to their pre-industrial levels, by ensuring the preservation of the already established 1500 acres of milkweed plantations, one of the last habitats for monarch butterflies during their migration to Canada.

This mission keeps us awake at night, literally, and we have been fortunate enough to have an army of passionate believers to push us from the beginning. Lasclay is a community. A community that loves milkweed and is just dedicated to preserve this wonderful plant, and all the wildlife that depends on it.

Every Lasclay product sold is a boost to the very hardworking milkweed growers who have weathered the storms of this fledgling industry since 2013. Every Lasclay product that leaves our facility is one more tremendous step toward preserving and even creating more milkweed fields, toward sustainably saving monarchs.
