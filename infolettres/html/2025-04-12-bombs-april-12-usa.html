Our annual nationwide milkweed planting campaign is in full swing across the country. To help the iconic and threatened monarch butterfly, thousands of new milkweed gardens will be planted this summer—and we’d love for you to join the movement!
 
Since our humble beginnings, we’ve always included a free packet of Common milkweed seeds in every order of our milkweed-insulated outdoor accessories. Due to growing demand, we’ve expanded our offerings to include other varieties like the stunning, bright orange Butterfly Milkweed (Asclepias tuberosa), the delicate purple-and-white Swamp Milkweed (Asclepias incarnata) and the gorgeous Showy Milkweed (asclepias speciosa).
However, we know that despite its benefits to biodiversity, milkweed can be a bit tricky to germinate. Most American milkweed species need a cold, moist treatment for at least 30 days, a process called “stratification”, followed by a few days of warmth (above 24°C/75°F), to break out of its dormancy. It's not hard, just long and a bit technical.
 
So, many people asked our help for their monarch garden —either by stratifying the seeds ourselves and offering them, or by providing rarer species and other flowers to feed adult monarchs.
 
We are very proud to launch a brand-new product to meet all those needs:
 
Milkweed & Wildflower Seed bombs! 🌼
 
 
This innovative product, inspired by Japanese traditions, combines enriched soil, a natural clay binder, and a mix of seeds (either various milkweed species or themed wildflowers). Pre-treated through cold stratification, these seed bombs are ready to plant upon arrival and can be used until August—making life a whole lot easier for pollinator gardeners. Just drop the bombs in planting cells or small pots, cover with clear plastic, leave near a window, and voilà! Germination guaranteed.
