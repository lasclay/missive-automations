2021: The big idea

From the very start of our company, we wanted to develop a summer product line and promote milkweed year-round, not just in the winter. The idea of a milkweed-insulated sleeping bag floated around, of course. It's a great idea that we haven't ruled out yet, but we were first looking for something more distinctive, more mainstream, that would fit into our strategy of small, everyday products where milkweed demonstrates its full insulating power and generates conversation. 

One day, after a lot of thinking, we had our breaktrough! Insulation also works the other way around... Milkweed can be used to insulate from the cold (like our mittens), but it can also be used to insulate the cold.

Keep the cold inside and the heat outside. Based on this basic, universal principle, we realized that milkweed could also be used as a thermal barrier... in a cooler! So in June 2021, we announced we were working on the first-ever 100% milkweed insulated cooler. Flexible but as efficient as a commercial hard cooler, this cooler, sporting a 100% hemp outer shell, could keep drinks and food cool during picnics at the park, days at the beach, a road trip or a day trek.


Our initial goal was to commercialize the flexible cooler in the Summer of 2022, provided that the final prototype passed the final stages. Which it did with flying colors... Except for the manufacturing validation stage. Indeed, while the prototype had proven its insulating power and had greatly impressed us, the first manufacturing cost estimate was less enchanting. 

The cooler in its current form had some major flaws:

  • It had too many pattern parts.
  • It required several difficult and time-consuming assembly steps.
  • It had a very long waterproof zipper as a closing mechanism. 

This brought the estimated production costs to more than $120 per cooler, which would have forced us to sell it at $250 to be profitable. Our mission being to democratize milkweed, it clearly didn't fit! You can't democratize much if you sell at two or three times the price of the competition! We had to go back to the drawing board and redesign the product, no matter what. 


2022: A sidestep, but the birth of another unique product

So in the Spring of 2022, we found ourselves unable to start any kind of flexible cooler production. It wasn't ready. A little embarrassed, we were about to just cancel the project for that year.

After a few days of weighing it all up, we came up with a very interesting solution. What if we made a milkweed cooler, but not out of fabric?

That way, we could postpone the flexible cooler to 2023, but still launch a Summer product in 2022 as promised. That's what we decided to do, using our technical expertise and inspired by a phrase we've often heard from our community:

"Mittens, scarves and coats are great... But we would love to actually see milkweed in a product!".

Wish granted.

In May 2022, we launched a 3D-printed milkeed cooler, with translucent walls through which we could finally see milkweed at work, with our own eyes!


This product, mixing natural materials and high technology, was truly a double demonstration of the power of milkweed: Not only can you go beyond winter products with this material, you can go beyond textile products altogether!

Launched as a pilot project in May 2022, over 100 were sold on the first day. We stopped there for a simple reason: 3D printing is an impressive and versatile technology, but not a very fast one. This video showing the production of a single cooler makes that pretty clear. Sped up to only 3 minutes long, the video lasts about 6 hours in its original version. So, 6 hours to 3D print the shell of a single cooler, not counting the time to fill and assemble... Let's just say it was a very innovative, very original product, but not a particularly profitable one. The 3D-printed version will therefore be discontinued and replaced by our new tote bag-sized cooler, which have the same function, can contain just as much but are more compact, economical and practical.
