Milkweed industry at a Crossroads?

We are truly at a turning point for milkweed in Canada. Of the 125 farmers who embarked on this great adventure back in 2014, barely more than twenty remain today.
 
Those who persist are holding on bravely and doubling down on innovation to make this eco-friendly and pioneering crop viable—one that also serves as a unique habitat for the endangered monarch butterfly. At Lasclay, we deeply believe in the future of milkweed as a world-class, flagship industry for Canada. The products and technologies we are developing at an accelerating pace continue to show, bit by bit, the incredible potential of this beautiful plant. But none of this would be possible without demand—without the support of our wonderful community.
 
Buying milkweed products means directly supporting the pioneering farmers behind this life-saving crop—for the monarch and for the marginal lands of our regions.
 
With this message, we hope to share some of the great hope that drives us forward and inspire our amazing community to support both us and the dedicated milkweed growers.
