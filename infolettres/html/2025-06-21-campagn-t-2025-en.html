With summer finally here, national holidays just around the corner, and vacation season kicking off, it’s the perfect time to slow down and enjoy. 🙂

It’s truly a season for making great memories—barbecues, picnics, sweltering days by the pool, evening campfires, toasted marshmallows, and those refreshing cold drinks that just hit the spot...

In our early days, summer was a quiet season for us, mostly focused on planning and design. After all, not too long ago, we only made milkweed-insulated winter gear like mittens and scarves!

Thanks to a lot of hard work and persistence, we’ve now developed over 30 milkweed-based products, including many designed for year-round use—or especially for summer. Lunch bags, backpack and messenger-style coolers, insulated sleeves that keep drinks cold for hours, oven and BBQ mitts... Milkweed silk, with its phenomenal insulating properties, deserves to be known and used in everyday life.

That’s our mission: to develop products and manufacturing technologies that showcase milkweed’s exceptional properties, while helping preserve and expand this innovative, eco-friendly crop that doubles as a critical habitat for monarch butterflies and other endangered pollinators. 🦋🐝

Together, we can bring this ambitious vision to life—while making the most of summer’s best moments. It goes by so fast, and maybe that’s why we love it so much. So, with that in mind, we’re happy to invite you to check out our summer milkweed collection. And if you think something might add to your summer joy, we’d be thrilled if you picked one up 😉👇
