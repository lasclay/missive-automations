Is there any greater joy than the return of warm weather, birds singing, and car shelters vanishing with the spring thaw?

With budding trees, fresh greenery, and longer days, we can’t help but get excited for summer—and start thinking about gardening.

Our national milkweed planting campaign is now celebrating its 5th anniversary, and it’s going strong! Each year, tens of thousands of new milkweed and native wildflower gardens are planted to support the monarch butterfly and native pollinators.
 
Participate in the national milkweed planting campaign
 
We’re incredibly proud of this movement—especially considering it all started quite simply. A few of our customers asked to buy larger quantities of the milkweed seed packets we used to give away for free with our milkweed silk insulated products.
 
We ran with the idea, and over the past five years, the campaign has grown step by step into something much bigger:
  • 2021: First milkweed planting campaign in Eastern Canada, featuring only common milkweed seeds sourced from our milkweed fiber fields.

  • 2022: Campaign expanded across all of Canada.

  • 2023: New milkweed varieties introduced: Swamp, Butterfly, and Showy Milkweed—more attractive and less invasive than common milkweed.

  • 2024: Launch of a new product—Native Seed Bombs, cold-stratified for 30 days to improve germination and simplify the user experience. Several new species of milkweed now available, including rare ones like Poke milkweed (asclepias exaltata)

  • 2025: Campaign expands across all of North America. And we’ve got 5 new milkweed seed bomb species! —see below!
 
Innovation doesn't stop. We now offer several species of milkweed and native wildflowers at different stages of growth. That means more people can plant pollinator gardens—no matter their level of gardening experience or how much time they have:
 

Milkweed Seed Packets

We offer 4 species in seed packets: Common milkweed (asclepias syriaca), Swamp milkweed (asclepias incarnata), Butterfly milkweed (asclepias tuberosa) and Showy milkweed (asclepias speciosa). To plant in spring, seeds need to undergo a cold stratification process. It’s simple (as shown in our guide here) but it does require a bit more time and patience!
Get milkweed seed packets

Milkweed Seed Bombs

These little balls contain a few milkweed seeds and have already been cold-stratified in our facilities. They come in 10 varieties now, ship by mail and are ready to use right when you receive them, even as late as August. A few consecutive days at room temperature (around 73–75°F) trigger germination. To maintain proper warmth and moisture, we suggest placing the bombs on top of moist potting soil in seed cells, covering them with a clear plastic lid or bag, and placing them in a sunny window to create a mini-greenhouse effect. Even the bag they ship in can temporarily do the trick—see the image below!
