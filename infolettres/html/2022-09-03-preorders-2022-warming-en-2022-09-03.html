September has already arrived! Now that the days are getting shorter and the nights colder, we hope you've enjoyed your summer as much as we did. It has been almost a year since our last presale... While the Earth was slowly completing its revolution around the Sun, ideas were born, developed and most importantly, materialized in our workshop.

In this email, the first of three in the coming weeks, we will begin to release some essential information about our much awaited yearly presale (date at the end of the email and new products below!).

In our first season back in 2020, the first Lasclay mittens were released and were met with a whirlwind of excitement, selling out in a matter of days. Last Fall, the demand for the mittens and our new product, the Lasclay scarf, was even stronger: in just 2 days, we matched the sales of the entire previous season.

New milkweed products
Maybe we are too optimistic, but this year we really hope this Cinderella story will continue. In addition to our two pioneering products (mittens and scarf), we are very proud to announce two new products for this season: a headband and a neck warmer, which will be unveiled with more details and pictures in the next email. Both products, just like their predecessors, are insulated with pure, organic milkweed floss, are meticulously designed and made in Canada mostly with North American materials and are both aesthetically pleasing and technically sound. 

Mittens 3.0
Once again this year, we reinvested all our profits in manufacturing innovation and automation but we also paid special attention to the design of our flagship product, the mittens! These efforts have had two very positive effects. First, our increased manufacturing efficiency allows us to sidestep inflation: our mittens will be sold at the same price as last year, $99 CAD and not a penny more. We want to prove that it is possible to keep making our products here in Canada and still be competitive. Secondly, we greatly improved the 2022 mittens, notably in terms of environmental impact. We replaced our China-made outershell and lining with two higher quality fabrics made in Canada and in the USA, respectively. Less transportation means less greenhouse gases and most importantly, reduced lead times which enable us to deliver preorders much faster!
