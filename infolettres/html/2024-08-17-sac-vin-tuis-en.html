We’re already halfway through August! We hope you're enjoying the end of summer just as much as we are. This season, we’re reflecting on all the progress that has been made on the democratization of milkweed as a responsible insulation alternative and the protection of wild pollinators/monarch butterflies.  
These mission are at the core of Lasclay’s business model, and would not be possible without the dedication and resilience of the pioneering milkweed farmers who,10 years ago, kickstarted this ambitious endeavor across Canada and the USA. These brave farmers are the backbone of our efforts to turn milkweed into a symbol of sustainable innovation.
Despite the challenges and obstacles that come with working closely with nature, our commitment to progress and innovation remains unwavering. In recent weeks, we’ve continued to push forward by developing four new milkweed-insulated products, which we are excited to reveal exclusively to our newsletter subscribers. 
 
Milkweed-Insulated Wine cooler Bag
This handy little product allows you to transport and keep wine bottles cool. It’s a great alternative to ice buckets that often leave water all over the table. It is equipped with a special strap that can be converted to a hand-carried handle or a shoulder strap, giving it a nice versatility!

Three Models of Insulated Electronic Device thermal sleeves
These insulated sleeves are designed for cell phones, tablets, or compact laptops and feature magnetic flaps for quick access and a small shoulder strap for the phone sleeve. Winter travel with devices in your backpack, or even a day of skiing, often leads to devices shutting down due to the cold, which can prematurely wear out their batteries. This new line of milkweed-insulated cases helps mitigate that problem. They’re also highly useful in the summer, as devices can shut down in the same way if exposed to the sun and overheating.
