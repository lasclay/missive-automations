We’re following up a little later than we would have liked regarding your Lasclay order containing urban mittens. They were originally scheduled to be completed and ready to ship by mid-November. We will have a slight delay on this timeline, mainly for two reasons:

1. Demand has been much higher than expected for this product and for our outdoor mittens, which are manufactured on the same production line.
 
2. We decided not to perform the final cut of the mitten liners with our automated laser cutters. While this method is very fast, the heat from the laser slightly melts synthetic fabrics, making the edges crusty. For this product, that would have made the mittens less comfortable to wear. It was noticeable at the fingertips, and we ultimately felt it wasn’t good enough to ship that way. So we chose to prioritize comfort, even if it means slowing production a little with manual cutting.
Jun Mark and Karen, 2 of our production employees, manually cutting the mittens in our Quebec City factory. Usually, we laser cut them and it's about 4x faster.
We now estimate delivery, conservatively, for the 1st or 2nd week of December. Rest assured, there’s virtually no chance we won’t deliver before Christmas — unless a meteorite falls on our workshop.
Almost everything is ready on our end — we only have one color left to cut.
After that, all that remains is the final assembly in Beauce. We have about 316 pairs to assemble, at 8 minutes each, so roughly 48 hours of work. We’re talking about a maximum of 2 weeks of delay if everything goes well!

We’re very sorry for this delay — we know the weather is getting colder, and we’re doing everything we can to move as fast as possible. Thank you so much for your trust and your patience.

Warmly,
