Is there any greater pleasure than the return of warm weather, humming birds, and the snow slowly melting down? With the Spring days getting longer, we look forward to summer and start thinking about gardening.

Our national milkweed planting campaign, now in its third year, is well underway. Thousands of new monarch butterfly gardens have been planted since 2021 and we are really, really proud of that! 

However, we must admit that common milkweed, the species we grow for its insulating silk, is not necessarily suitable for all needs. First, the seeds of all native milkweed species in Canada require a cold, wet stratification treatment of at least 30 days to germinate, a fairly challenging process for casual gardeners. Second, common milkweed itself presents landscaping challenges, as it spreads happily and ends up taking space that some people don't have, even to the backyards of neighbors who may not want it.

So several people have asked us to help them sprout their milkweed seeds and also to provide smaller and less aggressive native species such as butterfly milkweed (asclepias tuberosa), swamp milkweed (asclepias incarnata) or showy milkweed (asclepias speciosa). These three beautiful species are, however, more scarce and expensive, as no one grows them commercially. So we designed a new product to meet all these demands:

Milkweed "seed bombs"!

Our bombs are sold as divisible sticks in which enriched potting soil, a natural clay binder and your choice between four milkweed species are mixed together.

This product makes life so much easier for casual gardeners:

  1. When sowing: just drop the bombs on the surface, plain and simple. Since they are already coated with fertile soil, there is nothing more to do!

  2. Already cold stratified, these seed bombs are ready for planting when you receive them and can therefore be used until July or August, easily.

  3. To cover maximum surface, each of the bombs can be split into 4 cells each containing seeds.

  4. The flat and elongated shape of our bombs facilitates their storage in the fridge, taking very little space.
