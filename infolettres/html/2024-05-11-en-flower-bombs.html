We often hear that milkweed is the essential plant for saving monarch butterflies. This is 100% true, but there's an important nuance to be made. While the leaves of young milkweed plants are indeed the caterpillars' only food, adult monarch butterflies have a much more varied diet, gathering nectar from dozens of perennial and annual flowers. As milkweed only flowers for 3 to 4 weeks, monarchs need more during the summer, to store up the energy they need for their return to Mexico. The ideal habitat for a monarch butterfly is a meadow dotted with milkweed and a wide variety of nectar-producing flowers, which will ensure nectar availability from May to October.
