1. Native flowers seed bombs
We often hear that milkweed is the essential plant to save monarch butterflies. This is 100% true, but there is an important nuance to be made. While the leaves of young milkweed plants are indeed the only food for monarch caterpillars, adult butterflies have a much more diverse diet, sipping the nectar of dozens of perennial and annual flowers. Since milkweed only blooms for 3 to 4 weeks, monarchs need more during the summer to store up the energy they need and migrate back to Mexico. The ideal habitat for a monarch butterfly is a meadow brim with milkweed and a wide variety of indigenous flowers, which will ensure the availability of nectar from May to October. We started from our milkweed seed bomb concept and adapted it into native flower bombs, available in 6 themes: Edible, Hummingbirds & Pollinators, Melliferous, Renaturalization, Wetlands and Medicinal.

2. New milkweed species!
Several people asked us if it is possible to grow milkweed in a pot, on a balcony for example. Unfortunately, milkweeds native to Canada are not really suitable for pots and planters. As is the case for most perennials, these plants have deep root systems and need to be planted in the ground. But, we found a solution: Tropical milkweed! This is a beautiful tropical variety that is beloved by monarchs. A perennial plant in Mexico, it behaves as an annual in Canada. So you will have to replant it in the garden every summer, or grow it in a pot and bring it indoors during the winter to keep it alive. This is perfect for those who want to keep more control over their garden or those who live in apartments!
