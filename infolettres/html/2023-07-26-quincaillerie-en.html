We have finally started delivering the long-awaited coolers. We would like to clarify how to tighten the straps so as not to damage the hardware.

It's very simple: you need to gently adjust the straps by tightening them with both hands (one on the strap, the other on the hardware piece) so as not to put too much pressure on the plastic hardware.

Do not hesitate to write to us if you need more advice on your cooler.
