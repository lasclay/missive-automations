Production 

Production is well under way and going well: We have already manufactured 97% of the seed bombs ordered and put them in our refrigerators. We should reach 125% of the production this Tuesday.

Important reminder: The seed bombs are all scheduled to be delivered between the end of May and mid-June, as indicated on the product page of our online store. The reason for this is that in order to ensure satisfactory germination rates, the bombs must be cold treated in the refrigerator for 20 to 30 days, depending on the type of seed bombs.

This treatment, which we take care of before shipping, will allow you to plant your milkweed and/or your native perennials without any hassle, as soon as the night frosts are over. It is actually recommended to plant from the first week of June in order to eliminate the risk of night frost. So we are right on schedule!

Shipping 

Many people receive suspicious emails talking about a package to be picked up, customs to be paid or other. Just to clear up any doubts, we ship all our packages with Canada Post. If the email mentions UPS, DHL or other, it is not us. Normally, when your pre-order is officially shipped, an email signed by Lasclay will be sent to you automatically, with your order number starting with L-, and followed by 5 digits. 

Seed bomb orders of 10 units or less are shipped by regular mail, with a stamp. Once the "Your order is in transit" email is received, it can take between 3 and 9 business days to arrive, depending on the destination. For orders of more than 10 units, we ship a XpressPost package, which usually arrives in 2  to 4 business days.
